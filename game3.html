Minesweeper

A classic canvas Minesweeper with saved progress.

Reveal a square to start.

Click a revealed number when all its mines are flagged to open the remaining squares around it. Keyboard: focus the board, move with the arrow keys, press Enter or Space to act, and F to flag.

This game stores the current board in your browser's local storage so you can resume it. Game data never leaves your device. The site uses cookie-free, anonymous visit counting (details).
